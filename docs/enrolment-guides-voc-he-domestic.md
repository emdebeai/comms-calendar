# Enrolment Guides VOC — HE Domestic, thematic analysis

**Source:** Qualtrics intercept on the enrolment-guide pages (Student Journey: Enrol · page: Enrolment Guides), all time to 14 Aug 2026, Australia GeoIP. Filtered to the **HE domestic guide** (`/enrol-new-student/he-domestic`): **187 responses**, of which **~43 carry substantive free text** (the rest are scores only or empty follow-up artifacts). Verbatims quoted as written, incl. typos. Analysis and theme names by Claude.

**Framing:** the guides exist because Enrolment Online is difficult for some students to use. Accordingly, themes are split into **(A) system problems surfacing on the guide page** — things no guide edit can fix — and **(B) guide problems** the content team can act on. Roughly two-thirds of negative verbatim weight is (A).

Confidence is the weakest score — consistent with the theme profile below: students don't doubt the guide, they doubt they'll get through the system.

## A. System problems surfacing on the guide page

### A1. Login, credentials and the temporary password — the dominant theme (~13 of ~43, ≈30%)
Students land on the guide unable to complete step 1: the first login. Temporary passwords never arrive, aren't explained, or the login link goes somewhere unexpected. The guide gets the blame for a credential problem.

- "It does not tell me how to get a temporary password, so I cannot go past step 1" (Jul 2025)
- "how to find the temp password is particularly difficult to find. Including this in the enrolment email would make it easier" (Dec 2025)
- "CANT LOG IN. CANT ENROLL. CANT EVEN COMPLETE FIRST STEP NEED TO CONTACT IT SUPPORT" (Jan 2026)
- "I tried to sign into student enrolment with my new student address but it said: This username may be incorrect" (Jul 2026)
- "The most important link on this page is 'Log in to Enrolment Online'… it takes me to a microsoft login. It should be taking me to an RMIT web page!" (Sep 2025)
- "Include what an rmit password looks like since emails don't show it" (Jan 2026)
- Also: browser/cache login failures (Apr 2025), "password is being annoying" (Dec 2025), circular password resets after accepting a second offer (Apr 2026).

### A2. No unmistakable "enrol here" entry point — guide ↔ portal loop (~6, ≈14%)
Students bounce between the guide, emails and the portal without finding the actual door.

- "PLEASE INCLUDE SOME SORT OF ACTUAL LINK TO ENROLMENT AND NOT THIS PURGATORY ASS CIRCLE OF GETTING RELINKED BACK TO THE ENROLLMENT GUIDE" … "there's a big button at the top that says 'RMIT ONLINE' which feels mean when I am literally looking for where to enroll at RMIT online" (Jan 2026)
- "I click on the link from an email 'accept your offer' - and it takes me nowhere" (Oct 2025)
- "Make normal click to enroll why do I have to go thru different links just enroll" (Jul 2026)
- "Im trying to find where I enrol and how to." (Jan 2026)

### A3. The underlying process is long and complex (~5, ≈12%)
The guide describes a process students experience as too long; several conflate offer acceptance, account creation and enrolment into one exhausting flow.

- "It has taken me 4 hours to try to enrol so far hot spotting from a campsite. Absolutely ludicrous" (Jan 2026)
- "The whole accepting/deferring an offer is quite complex… I'm having to flip back to my acceptance email and videos" / "too many steps in the whole accepting offer/deferring offer process" (Jan 2026)
- "overly complicated process when I've already received an offer" (Dec 2025)
- "very slow process" (Jul 2026)

### A4. Offer actions (accept / decline / defer) are tangled into enrolment (~3)
- "if someone is wanting to defer, decline or accept an offer just make it simple. I have been trying to figure out how to decline my offer and there is no direct answer anywhere and emailing for help did not work" (Feb 2026)

### A5. No human fallback (~3 verbatims; 10 of 187 respondents chose "looking for someone to speak to")
- "I can't find anyone to help me online with this enrolment" (Jul 2026)
- "I could not find in the page where to go to speak to someone about enrolment" (Oct 2025)

## B. Guide problems — actionable by the content team

### B1. Instructions not stepped / not scannable (~9, ≈21%)
The most actionable guide theme: students want a numbered checklist, plainer sentences, clearer subheadings, jump links, key items grouped instead of scattered down a long page.

- "Have clear and stepped instructions" / "Very unclear instructions" (Jul 2026)
- "The process is confusing. Consider creating a user friendly checklist" (Jul 2026)
- "make it less confusing by making sentences easier to understand." (Jan 2026)
- "too complicated, too much information. better to have it stepped out on separate pages or something" (Dec 2025)
- "Clearer subheadings" (Jan 2026) · "Hyperlinks to jump down the page to relevant sections" (Jan 2026) · "making the important or the key items more visible and in one place instead of scrolling" (Jan 2026)
- "Might be helpful is this information was also a downloadable PDF (?)" (Jul 2025)

### B2. Guide doesn't match what students actually see (~3)
- "The image of the buttons to press to enrol in a subject showed me my page did not have this. One thing I found confusing was the area and number for subject. If a subject has COSC49293, it isnt clear these are the two inputs i need." (Jan 2026)
- "Only suits students starting out, when it is also used by students enrolling for their next courses who are already in a program" (Jan 2026)

### B3. Guide doesn't answer "what should I enrol in" (~3)
The guide covers *how* to enrol, not *what* to choose — but that's the question some arrive with.

- "What subjects should i enrol, and can i enrol in subject from other faculty 1st semester… Would really like to have my question answered" (Nov 2025)
- "The class selection part of the guide was somewhat confusing, I'm still not certain I did it right." (Jan 2026)

### B4. Edge needs (~2)
- Dyslexic student caught out by FEE-HELP assumptions: "It would be good for Neuro Diverse students - to have specific assistance, or a section on the website to help them navigate the admin side of being a student" (Dec 2025)
- "more design, its simple and easy but bland" (Dec 2025) — visual polish is a non-issue here compared with program pages.

## Counter-evidence

- "the guide attached to the website is very helpful. Looking forward to starting my university journey at RMIT" (Dec 2025)
- "it was straight forward" (Apr 2025) · "It's amazing experience" (Aug 2025)
- "I would say it is laid out to be functional, which is what is needed." (Sep 2025)

Most scores sit at 3–4; the loud negatives cluster at the moments the system locks someone out.

---

## Read-out

1. **The guide is carrying blame for the system.** The top theme by far is credentials/login (A1) — invisible to a content team looking only at guide copy, but it's what students are on the guide page trying to solve. The single highest-leverage fix named by students themselves: **put the temporary password (or exactly where to find it) in the enrolment email**, and make the guide's login link land on an RMIT-branded page.
2. **One unmistakable door.** A persistent, prominent "Enrol now → Enrolment Online" action on the guide would kill the A2 loop; several verbatims describe circling between guide, email and portal.
3. **Guide fixes are cheap and specific:** numbered checklist format, plain-English sentences, jump links/anchors, screenshots that match the current UI, subject-code explanation (area + number), and a visible continuing-student path.
4. **The "what do I choose" gap (B3) belongs to the Experience Layer** — it is Stage 9's Map question ("Which subjects do I need to choose for my first semester?") showing up verbatim in live VOC. This dataset closes the Stage 9 evidence gap flagged in the Experience Layer doc.
