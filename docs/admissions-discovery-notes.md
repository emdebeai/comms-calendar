# Admissions discovery notes

Raw findings from the Admissions conversation (Aug 2026). What's mapped for
the current persona vs. deferred for future personas.

## Ruling for persona-01 (DOM SL · VTAC · no selection task)

**One outbound Admissions comm only: the Offer Round 1 letter.**
The most common journey applies through VTAC and hits none of the selection
task / scheme / pathway flows below. Mapped as:

- "Congratulations (name), you're in!" — 23 Dec (Offer Round 1), sent from
  Marketo. CTAs: Go to your Enrolment Guide / Defer your offer / Contact
  Student Connect. Admissions sends Conversion the VTAC offers file the same
  day, which sets off the Conversion post-offer sequence (SMS same day after
  2pm → EDM1 +2wks → SMS2 +2wks → Last Day to Enrol EDM).

## How offers work (context)

- VTAC gives Admissions the timelines and the offer file; it's loaded into
  Marketo (offer letters are Marketo emails).
- ~90% of letter content is identical across programs.
- Offer rounds: **Dec round** carries the bulk (~8,000 school-leaver offers),
  then Jan (×2, ~2,000–3,000 across the next rounds) and Feb (×2–3) — eight
  offer rounds in total.
- Admissions and Conversion work closely through offer round: offer file
  handed over, SMS goes the same day.
- All inbound student contact goes via Study@RMIT.

## Deferred — future personas / variants

- **Selection tasks (RAS):** ~30 of 200+ programs (art & design mainly).
  Applicants apply/submit direct in RAS → triggered comms: registration
  confirmation, reminders to submit, selection-task received confirmation,
  indicative offer / outcome. A "DOM SL VTAC + selection task" variant would
  add these Admissions touchpoints pre-offer.
- **RAS-direct applicants:** full RAS-triggered flow (register confirmation,
  reminders, outcomes), NSL Marketo comms for packaged pathways / process
  explainers, enrolment letters as they progress, conditional-offer process.
- **Ngara Willam:** manages Indigenous access — dedicated comms to Indigenous
  applicants; scheme touchpoints happen pre-offer rounds.
- **Access schemes generally** (Indigenous access, elite athletes): offer
  letters are the same, but applicants apply through the schemes pre-offer
  and those schemes have their own touchpoints.
- **NSL early offer** (separate journey).
