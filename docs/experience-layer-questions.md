### Six persistent gaps — domestic prospective, undergraduate [Dovetail · 2024–2026]

The same six themes recur across both the 2024 and 2025–26 VOC eras, filtered to domestic + undergraduate prospective:

1. **Fees & cost clarity**
2. **Entry requirements & admissions** — ATAR / entry scores, prerequisites and selection criteria visible per course, in one place.
3. **Career outcomes** — what jobs / careers a degree leads to; careers mapped to specialisations.
4. **Course structure & subject detail** — concrete subjects, majors/minors, electives and learning outcomes, not generic overviews.
5. **Navigation, findability & page design** — hard to find specific info; dated visual design; colour, hierarchy, larger fonts.
6. **Intake & application dates** — *moderate evidence.* When the course starts and when applications open / close.

*Caveat on ranking:* Dovetail labels fees "strongest, most persistent" across the whole folder, but in this persona's cut (Aug 2025–26, domestic current high-school) fees is 3.1% of verbatims — behind visual design, ATAR and content depth. The folder-wide ranking likely reflects VE and concession fees from broader segments, so it is not treated as the top theme for this persona.

*Counter-evidence / balance:* "a meaningful minority praise the pages as clear and easy to navigate," and the newer pathway diagrams "drew unprompted praise from a careers practitioner." The themes are improvement requests layered on a generally positive base, not signs of a failing experience.
